Model Fleet

A Fully Local, Multimodal AI Platform

Models in Production

  • Language: two Mixture-of-Experts and two dense LLMs, including Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Qwen3.8-27B with multi-token prediction, each tuned for its use case.
  • Image and video: Ideogram 4.0 for images, LTX-2.3 for video.
  • Voice: Whisper Large-v3 and SenseVoice for speech recognition, emotion2vec+ for speech emotion, and Kokoro for text-to-speech.
  • Every model's license was reviewed to confirm commercial use before deployment.

Serving, Security, and Access

  • llama.cpp (Vulkan / ROCm / CUDA builds) and vLLM, behind a LiteLLM gateway.
  • LiteLLM is the security and access layer: per-user keys, guardrails, and routing each workload to the right model.
  • Served to US and India teams through Open WebUI over Tailscale.
  • Zero external cloud AI calls.
