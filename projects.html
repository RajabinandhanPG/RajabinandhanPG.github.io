Project 01

Hand-Built Multi-GPU Inference Platform

Technical Summary

  • Self-assembled AMD EPYC and Ryzen hosts with dual R9700, V620, and RTX 5090 GPUs.
  • Two-host Proxmox cluster with GPU passthrough into isolated LXC containers.
  • Two Mixture-of-Experts and two dense LLMs served via llama.cpp and vLLM.
  • A LiteLLM gateway handles per-user keys, guardrails, and routing to the right model.

Why It Matters

  • Handles 300–500M tokens a month across 20 users.
  • Cut monthly AI spend from $4K–$5K to about $700–$900, roughly 80% lower.
  • No sensitive prompt or document ever leaves owned hardware.
  • Every model's license was reviewed for commercial use before deployment.

Project 03

Home-Health Route Optimization and Contract RAG

Route Optimization Engine

Modelled day on real road networks with synthetic workforce data.

  • Placed 51 visits, versus 45 planned manually.
  • Drove 45% fewer miles and 232 fewer minutes.
  • Every visit it couldn't place came with a stated reason.
  • Respects clinician qualifications, patient time windows, and shift capacity.

Contract Intelligence Architecture

An on-premises RAG design; the architecture is complete.

  • Clause-boundary chunking, so definitions stay attached to their sub-clauses.
  • pgvector and full-text search in one Postgres database, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • Access-control and effective-date pre-filters before ranking.
  • Every answer cites the exact clause, and the system says "not found" rather than guessing.
