What the Role Covers

Scope of Responsibility

I established and lead the IT function across home health, nursing, transport, medical equipment, and education businesses.

  • Support 300+ users across five operating companies.

Building With What Exists

I default to strengthening existing platforms and in-house automation before adding new vendors.

  • Replaced a paid SaaS messaging vendor with in-house Apps Script and Dialpad automation that non-technical staff run themselves.
  • Use APIs and automation for routine coordination instead of adding manual work.
  • Plan rollouts in stages so care operations never stop.

Governance, Hardening, and Incident Response

Compartmentalized Operations

  • Designed and enforced compartmentalized endpoint, identity, and operational environments across all five entities.
